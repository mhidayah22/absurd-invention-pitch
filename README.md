One ridiculous invention in one sentence, 152 characters: a toothbrush that
unionises with the toothpaste, goes on strike every Monday, and only comes back
after a formal apology and a pay rise paid in mint flavour.

> My toothbrush unionised with the toothpaste, went on strike every Monday, and only came back after a formal apology and a pay rise paid in mint flavour.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the line (one sentence, at most 220 characters) and writes it to
`entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
